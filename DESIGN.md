## Brand & Style

The brand identity embodies architectural authority, structural integrity, and contemporary Korean craftsmanship. Designed for high-end residential, commercial renovation, and structural engineering clients, this design system projects permanence, transparency, and meticulous precision.

The visual style is **Corporate Modern with Architectural Minimalism**. It synthesizes monolithic layouts, structural grids, crisp tectonic line work, and generous negative space reminiscent of blueprint draftsmanship and contemporary gallery exhibitions. Rather than leaning on superficial decorations or playful rounded motifs, the interface relies on deliberate typographic rhythm, razor-sharp alignment, balanced slate tonalities, and purposeful micro-interactions that communicate reliability, fiscal solidity, and engineering mastery.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in enduring architectural elements:

- **Primary (`#1B3B6F`)**: An authoritative, deep architectural navy conveying corporate stewardship, heavy civil engineering discipline, and structural permanence.
- **Secondary (`#3182F6`)**: A precise electric slate blue used purposefully for interactive wayfinding, active states, status highlights, and conversion triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#C2A87E`)**: A warm limestone and aged brass accent that introduces tactile luxury, tailored interior renovation warmth, and natural materiality.
- **Neutral (`#171717`)**: Dense iron charcoal serving as high-contrast structural text and authoritative section dividers, avoiding pitch-black fatigue while maintaining commanding legibility.

Background surfaces rely on crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) framed against balanced light limestone grays (`#F8F8F8` and `#EDEDED`), mirroring plaster, precast concrete panels, and architectural models.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial hierarchy with an architectural cadence.

- **Display & Headlines (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Provides clean geometry, assertive modernist proportions, and high readability across broad digital canvases. Tight negative letter-spacing ensures titles look chiseled and structural.
- **Body & Data Prose (`Inter`)**: Serves as the primary workhorse, offering optimal optical performance for bilingual Korean/English text strings, contractual documentation, and project case study descriptions.
- **Blueprint & Technical Specifiers (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Leveraged for project serial numbers, square footage, CAD coordinates, dates, and status tags to evoke technical mastery and drafting board accuracy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements a structured 12-column architectural grid on desktop (1440px max-width container), reflowing to an 8-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column stack on mobile. 

Whitespace is treated as a structural material: wide margins and rigorous gutters reflect the calm, intentional spacing of contemporary master-planned architecture. Alignments favor rigid horizontal datums and visible vertical axes. Content blocks must lock strictly to grid lines, avoiding loose or floating alignments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes **Low-Contrast Outlines & Tonal Tiers** over theatrical drop shadows, reflecting the physicality of framed glass, concrete slabs, and steel mullions:

1. **Base Tier (Plinth)**: Pure flat `#FFFFFF` and light concrete `#F8F8F8` defining zones through full-bleed horizontal line work (`1px solid #E2E4E8`).
2. **Structural Panels (Cards & Modules)**: Delimited with sharp 1px borders in `#E2E4E8` or dark boundary lines `#171717`.
3. **Floating Overlays (Modals & Quote Drawers)**: When elevation is mandatory, shadows remain ambient and minimal: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.08)`, paired with a fine 1px perimeter border.
4. **Interactive Hover**: Cards transition with a subtle inward border shift to primary navy or a clean 2px bottom accent rule rather than bouncy vertical lifts.

## Shapes

Shapes utilize a refined **Soft (0.25rem / 4px)** baseline. This microscopic chamfer softens harsh aliasing while preserving the taut, disciplined silhouette of engineered building materials. Circular pills and exaggerated rounds are strictly prohibited to ensure the brand feels dignified, structural, and architecturally deliberate.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#1B3B6F` navy container, pure white typography (`Hanken Grotesk`, medium/semi-bold), 4px radius. 12px 24px padding. Hover deepens to `#142C53` with smooth 150ms transitions.
- **Secondary Action**: Transparent background with a 1px border in `#171717`, dark charcoal typography. Hover fills with `#171717` and inverts text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text & Link Actions**: Monospaced technical label paired with an inline directional arrow, accented by an underline that expands outward on hover.

### Portfolio Cards & Project Galleries
- **Structure**: Framed within a 1px `#E2E4E8` grid. High-resolution architectural photography top-aligned with an aspect ratio of 16:10 or 4:3.
- **Content Block**: Lower section houses a monospaced category label (`label-blueprint` in `#C2A87E`), followed by the project name in `title-md`, completion year, and gross area metrics rendered in crisp data columns.

### Quote Inquiry Forms & Inputs
- **Input Fields**: Crisp rectangular inputs with `#FFFFFF` background, a 1px border in `#E2E4E8`, and 4px radius. On focus, transitions cleanly to a 1px high-contrast `#1B3B6F` border without outer fuzzy halos.
- **Labels**: Rendered above the field in `body-sm` bold, complemented by optional format hints using `label-blueprint`.
- **Select & Checkbox Controls**: Checkboxes feature square edges (2px radius) with an architectural solid fill `#1B3B6F` and white check icon when active.

### Segmented Tabs & Filters
- **Style**: Flush horizontal rule with tab headers separated by vertical thin rules or balanced horizontal padding. Active tabs feature a bottom bar in solid `#1B3B6F` (2px) and weighted font, eschewing bubbly bubble tabs for blueprint-style index dividers.

### Data Tables (Specifications & Feasibility)
- **Table Anatomy**: Clean alternating rows between `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F8F8`. Headers displayed in `label-blueprint` uppercase with subtle letter-spacing and a definitive bottom border in `#171717`. Numeric and cost values strictly right-aligned using `JetBrains Mono`.
